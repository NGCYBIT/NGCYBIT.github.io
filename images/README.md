# Image filenames

Place your images in this folder. File names are case-sensitive.

| File | Location | Suggested aspect ratio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LOGO.png | Website favicon | Square, at least 64 × 64 |
| home-hero.jpg | Home hero | Approximately square |
| about-platform.jpg | Home feature and About | Square or 16:10 |
| insight-01.jpg | User-centered trading article | 16:9 |
| insight-02.jpg | Market information article | 16:9 |
| insight-03.jpg | Interface experience article | 16:9 |
| insight-04.jpg | Trading infrastructure article | 16:9 |
| insight-05.jpg | Products and services article | 16:9 |
| insight-06.jpg | User education article | 16:9 |
| insight-07.jpg | User support article | 16:9 |
| insight-08.jpg | Responsible operations article | 16:9 |
| insight-09.jpg | Technology and participation article | 16:9 |
| insight-10.jpg | Long-term development article | 16:9 |

The page layouts show abstract artwork until these optional images are supplied. Your image replaces that artwork automatically when it loads. It may be cropped to fit the layout. Use original brand assets, no people or contact details if that is your image brief. The textual NGCYBIT header is a wordmark label, not a reconstruction of your supplied logo.

If you prefer PNG or WebP article images, update the matching .jpg references in the HTML files. Keep the favicon filename LOGO.png exactly as written.
